IATA Baggage Regulations & Dangerous Goods Policy

Ensure all baggage complies with IATA international carriage standards and Star Alliance dimensions prior to airport departure.

Checked Baggage Policy (IATA Piece Concept)

Each checked baggage piece must not exceed 23 kg (50 lbs) in Economy Class or 32 kg (70 lbs) in Cloud Nine Business Class. Maximum linear dimensions (Length + Width + Height) must not exceed 158 cm (62 inches). Baggage exceeding standard weight or dimensional allowances will incur applicable IATA excess baggage surcharges at the check-in desk.

Cabin Baggage Allowances (Hand Baggage)

Economy passengers are permitted one (1) piece of cabin baggage not exceeding 55 × 40 × 23 cm (22 × 16 × 9 inches) and 7 kg (15 lbs), plus one personal item (such as a laptop case, small briefcase, or handbag) that fits securely under the seat forward. Cloud Nine passengers may transport two (2) cabin bags of up to 8 kg (18 lbs) each.

IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations (DGR) & Restricted Articles

In accordance with ICAO Technical Instructions and IATA DGR, spare lithium-ion batteries, e-cigarettes, personal vaporizers, and portable power banks must be carried exclusively within cabin baggage and are strictly prohibited inside checked baggage. Liquids, Aerosols, and Gels (LAGs) in cabin baggage must be in individual containers not exceeding 100 ml (3.4 fl oz) placed within a single transparent, resealable plastic bag of maximum 1 litre capacity.
