IATA Route Regulations & International Fare Conditions

Standard regulatory conditions governing scheduled international passenger air services from Addis Ababa Bole (ADD).

Fare Validity, Surcharges & Price Guarantee

Quoted fares represent total all-inclusive passenger prices comprising base airfare, government aviation security charges, international passenger service facility charges (PSC), and airline fuel surcharges. Selected fares and seat inventory are secured for 15 minutes during checkout.

International Transit Clearances & Transfer Protocols

Passengers holding itineraries with connections through Dubai International (DXB), Hamad International (DOH), or Frankfurt (FRA) benefit from automated through-checked baggage transfer to destination. Travelers must ensure compliance with intermediate transit visa and national immigration health entry requirements.

Involuntary Schedule Disruption & Passenger Rights (Montreal Convention Framework)

In accordance with the Montreal Convention (1999) and IATA Recommended Practices, involuntary carrier-initiated flight cancellations, diversions, or schedule delays exceeding three (3) hours entitle passengers to priority re-accommodation on the next available flight or a full involuntary refund without penalty.
