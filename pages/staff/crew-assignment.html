Assignment notes

Checks required before confirmation

  • Every assigned crew member must be available for the selected flight date and time.
  • Pilot and cabin crew qualifications must match the aircraft and assigned role.
  • License and medical clearance dates must be valid for the duty period.
  • Assigned crew must not have overlapping flights or conflicting duty periods.
  • Required rest periods and applicable duty-time limits must be respected.

HTML phase note: These are the business rules the later crew controller must validate. This page currently provides the form structure only; it does not yet perform automatic conflict or qualification checks.
