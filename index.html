International Travel Guidelines & Passenger Policies

Standard regulatory requirements governing international air carriage, luggage conveyance, and passenger rights.

IATA Standard Baggage Allowance Policy (Piece Concept)

Standard Economy ticket holders are entitled to two (2) pieces of checked baggage weighing up to 23 kg (50 lbs) each, with maximum combined linear dimensions (Length + Width + Height) not exceeding 158 cm (62 inches). Cabin baggage is limited to one (1) piece not exceeding 55 × 40 × 23 cm (max 7 kg / 15 lbs) plus one small personal item (such as a slim laptop bag or handbag) stowed beneath the seat forward.

Airport Check-in Deadlines & Boarding Gate Closures

Web and mobile check-in opens 24 hours prior to Scheduled Time of Departure (STD) and closes strictly 90 minutes prior. For international departures, passengers must report to the airport check-in counter at least three (3) hours prior to STD. In compliance with aviation security regulations, boarding gates close strictly 20 minutes before departure.

Ticket Rescheduling, Voluntary Cancellation, & Fare Refund Conditions

Cancellations requested 24 hours or more before scheduled departure qualify for partial refund subject to fare family rules. Economy Flex and Business Class fares permit complimentary date changes (fare difference may apply). Involuntary delays exceeding three hours or flight cancellations are governed by Montreal Convention passenger rights.

Special Service Requests (SSR), Dietary Requirements, & Mobility Assistance

Special meals (such as Halal, Kosher, Hindu Vegetarian, Diabetic, or Gluten-Intolerant options) must be requested at least 24 hours prior to departure via the booking portal. Wheelchair assistance (WCHR, WCHS, WCHC) and unaccompanied minor (UMNR) services should be arranged during ticket reservation.
